Malayali Common English Pitfalls & Fixes

1. "Yesterday only / Now only"
❌ Don't say: "I reached yesterday only."
✅ Say: "I reached just yesterday." or "I arrived yesterday."
This happens because in Malayalam we add '-ആണ്' ('ഇന്നലെയാണ്'). In English, use 'just' or leave out 'only'.
2. "I have a doubt"
❌ Don't say: "Teacher, I have a doubt."
✅ Say: "Teacher, I have a question." or "Could you clarify this?"
'Doubt' implies suspicion or disbelief. For learning, always use 'question'.
3. "Myself Rahul"
❌ Don't say: "Myself Rahul from Kochi."
✅ Say: "I am Rahul from Kochi." or "My name is Rahul."
Reflexive pronouns like 'Myself' cannot stand as the subject of a sentence.
4. "He didn't came"
❌ Don't say: "He didn't came yesterday."
✅ Say: "He didn't come yesterday."
Once you use 'did' or 'didn't', the main verb must always be in base form ('come', not 'came').
